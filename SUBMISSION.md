Title: Agent Handoff Check

Notes / Description:
Built a reusable task-custody ledger. Owners freeze requirements, the current holder proposes a named receiver with completed work, evidence, and open work, and GenLayer validators produce a bounded coverage mask plus readiness signal. The receiver alone accepts or rejects custody, every accepted handoff is retained, and the owner can close only after a real transfer. No funds or external systems are controlled.
